How it works

🎁 Claim daily

Sign in with your Starknet wallet and claim once a day. It resets at 00:00 UTC. Each claim gives 20 XP, plus 5 more for every day of your streak (up to 70 XP).

🔥 Streak bonuses

Big extra XP when your streak hits day 3 (+30), 7 (+100), 14 (+150), 30 (+500), 60 (+800), 100 (+2,000) and 365 (+10,000).

🧊 Streak freeze

Miss one day and your weekly freeze saves the streak automatically (one per week, Monday to Sunday). Miss two days in a row and it starts again from day 1.

⭐ Levels

XP adds up to your account level, shown on your profile and next to your wallet. Levels 10 and 25 earn badges of their own.

🏅 Badges

Five ways to earn them: daily activity, holding $DEGENZ, burning on the bonfire, racing in Degenz Kart and using the portfolio. Tap any badge to see what it takes.

💎 Rarity

Every badge is Common, Silver, Gold or Trophy, shown by the colour of its ring. Once earned, a badge is yours for good, even if you later sell.

🏎️ Kart trails

Some badges unlock a trail that streams behind your kart in Degenz Kart. Pick it in the game's Settings; everyone in your online room sees it.

  • 🔸 Ember: First Howl (your first claim)
  • 🔥 Wildfire: Week Wolf (7-day streak)
  • 🌈 Rainbow: Iron Howl (30-day streak)
  • 🐺 Gold Wolf: Wolf (hold 1M $DEGENZ)
  • 👑 Royal: Pack Leader (hold 10M $DEGENZ)
  • 🪵 Ashes: Kindling (burn any $DEGENZ)
  • ⚡ Neon: Combo King (10× combo in Degenz Kart)
ℹ️ Good to know
  • Holding and burning badges only count the wallet you signed in with, not other wallets in your portfolio.
  • They're checked on-chain and update within about 10 minutes of buying or burning.
  • Racing badges count when you play Degenz Kart in the same browser where you're signed in on this site.
  • Signing in is a free signature: no transaction, and nothing leaves your wallet.
